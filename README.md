# TargetMart & Haarplaneet

## About

TargetMart and Haarplaneet are Dutch online retail platforms offering a diverse selection of products for customers in the Netherlands and beyond.

## TargetMart

[Visit TargetMart](https://www.targetmart.nl/)

TargetMart is an online store specializing in international food and world products. Customers can discover products from different cuisines and cultures, including American, Asian, Indian and other international food products, alongside household, kitchen and related products.

TargetMart aims to make international products accessible and convenient for customers through online shopping.

## Haarplaneet

[Visit Haarplaneet](https://www.haarplaneet.nl/)

Haarplaneet is an online specialist in Afro-American cosmetics and hair care, with a focus on curly, coily and textured hair.

The store offers a wide range of shampoos, conditioners, styling products, hair treatments, oils, vitamins and other hair-care products from many established brands.
